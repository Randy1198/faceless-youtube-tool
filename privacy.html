1. Overview

Welcome to FacelessYouTubeTool.com ("we", "our", "us"). We are committed to protecting your online privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we collect, how it is used, and how third-party advertising networks interact with your browsing session.

FacelessYouTubeTool.com operates as a client-side JavaScript web application. All text generations, script formatting calculations, and prompt options run directly inside your web browser. We do not require account registration, nor do we store or harvest your generated scripts on our servers.

Like most modern websites, we use cookies to optimize user experience. Cookies are small text files stored on your device. Third-party vendors, including advertising networks, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites across the internet.

We partner with third-party ad networks (such as Google AdSense, Adcash, and affiliated networks) to display banners and interactive ads when you visit our site. These partners may use cookies, JavaScript, and web beacons to measure ad effectiveness and personalize ad content.

  • Third-party vendors use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info or adjusting your browser cookie settings.

5. Log Files

FacelessYouTubeTool.com follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamp, referring/exit pages, and number of clicks. These are not linked to any personally identifiable information.
